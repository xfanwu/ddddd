Why it's nice

  • Nothing to set up. The downloader (aria2) lives inside the app. Open it and paste.
  • Files that open. Safe defaults stop the silent corruption big downloads suffer elsewhere, and it never overwrites your files.
  • Videos too. X (Twitter), TikTok, Instagram and about 1,750 other sites.
  • Several at once. Each download gets its own little beetle lane.
  • Private. No account, no cookies, no telemetry.

FAQ

How do I download a video from X (Twitter), TikTok or Instagram on Mac or Windows?

Copy the post's link and paste it into Dung. Video support is a free one-click add-on the first time.

Do I need Homebrew, aria2 or anything else?

No. The downloader is built into Dung. Install it and paste a link.

Is Dung free and private?

Yes. Dung is open source (MIT), with no account, no cookies and no telemetry.

Can Dung resume large downloads?

Yes. Interrupted downloads pick up where they left off, and large files download in safe parallel pieces.

macOS says it can't open Dung. What now?

Dung is free and not notarized by Apple. Right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again. On Windows, choose More info → Run anyway.
