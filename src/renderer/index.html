What went missing?

Dung ships with aria2, a free, open-source downloader that does the actual fetching. If it gets removed or stops starting, Dung can't download. On Windows, Fix it downloads the official aria2 build again; on Mac, reinstalling Dung restores it. Nothing else on your computer is changed.

Just press ⌘V — it starts by itself.
